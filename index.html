How do you reliably deploy an LLM into a domain where being wrong is expensive? This is the core question throughout my research, which focused on evaluation, domain-specialized modeling, retrieval-augmented generation and agent serving. Building a live legal service, I realized that what a model's technical report tells you is not what its users experience. Closing that gap starts with being able to say where a model fails, not just how often.
